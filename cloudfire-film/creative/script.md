## Script decisions

- **78 words**, against a target of 85–105. The first timed read ran Act III at 82 % speech density, so the Experience & Growth line ("The hardest journey is redesigned until it feels obvious.") was cut and kept in `narration.json → alternates`. Its visual carries the idea, and the silence lets the score build into the proof.
- No metrics, no client claims beyond the verified register, no "We don't just…" constructions, no sentence-fragment drumrolls, and no futurism.
- Narration never describes what the picture already shows. It names the stakes (a promise kept), the thesis (rarely on the screen), the method (both sides; a person approves; nobody holds their breath), the evidence (files keep coming back, verified) and the philosophy (it simply holds).
- Pronunciation: AegisStore = /ˈiːdʒɪs stɔː/ (EE-jis store); CloudFire = /ˈklaʊd faɪə/.
