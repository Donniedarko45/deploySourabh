# Production report: "Beneath the Surface"

CloudFire IT Services launch film · prepared 2026-10-08

## 1. Status, in one paragraph

A complete 65-second film exists and every deliverable passed technical validation (section 3). The picture is final: every frame is rendered natively at 3840 × 2160, 24 fps, by a deterministic compositor. The product footage is genuine, captured from a local build of AegisStore, including a real node failure. The score and sound design are original and complete. **The narration is a temporary local voice** (Kokoro v1.0, `bm_george`). The brief's Gemini TTS, ElevenLabs Music and OpenRouter image/video models were **not used**: no API keys were configured, and the session's network policy blocked `openrouter.ai`, `api.elevenlabs.io`, `huggingface.co` and the live `www.cloudfireitservices.com`. The adapters for all of them are written and budget-gated. Nothing was spent. On the internal creative rubric the film scores a mean of 7.9/10 against a target of 9 (section 7). The gap is named honestly, and most of it closes with a directed voice.

Resolution notes:
- The 16:9 picture is native 4K. Nothing is upscaled except the MODULX page (S12), whose only available capture is 1440 px wide. It is shown at about 1.2–1.4× on screen; a fresh capture was not possible because `modulx.in` is blocked from this environment.
- The vertical edition is a native 1080 × 1920 render of a recomposed 9:16 version of the scene, not a crop. Cameras are re-framed per shot, the S10 journey runs as a column, the node ring is reoriented, and the end card stacks.
- The H.264 master is quality-targeted (CRF 18, capped at 10.5 Mbps, so it fits GitHub's 100 MB file limit). The ProRes 422 HQ master (`.mov`, 6.0 GB) is the high-quality editing master. It is not committed to git (size), so it exists only in the production environment and can be regenerated with `tools/edit/deliver.sh`.

3. QA tooling found and fixed two of its own faults: an ASR chunker that split mid-sentence (it now splits only inside pauses of at least 250 ms) and a one-frame seek offset in frame extraction.

## 8. Remaining limitations

- Narration is temporary (local TTS). Swapping it needs only `GEMINI_API_KEY`; the steps are in the README.
- The live website could not be visited from this environment (403). The audit used the repository's local production build, and `docs/verification.md` notes that the public domain served the former site when last checked.
- MODULX: the source is 1440 px (scaled about 1.2–1.4×), and the client's permission for a promotional film is unconfirmed.
- The score and mix have not been heard by a human. Every judgement about them comes from measurement: loudness, spectral balance, ASR intelligibility (WER 0 on the full mix and on a phone-speaker fold-down), and sync.
- The ProRes master is not in git because of its size.
- The film is rendered on CPU (SwiftShader), so depth of field is approximated per element rather than physically. No generative imagery appears anywhere in the picture.
